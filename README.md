**AETHER CLASH** — an original arcane card duel for the browser. Two duelists,
30 HP each, a mana pool that grows every turn, and a 24-card deck of Aether
creatures and spells. Play creatures to your board, attack with them once they
are ready (Guard creatures must be dealt with first), and burn the enemy
duelist down to 0 HP before they do the same to you.

## How to play

- Click a card in your hand to play it (mana permitting).
- Click one of your ready creatures, then an enemy creature or the enemy
  duelist to attack. Creatures deal damage to each other simultaneously.
- End Turn hands over to the AI duelist. Number keys 1–7 select a hand card,
  Enter ends the turn, Esc cancels an attack selection.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output node main.ts   # hands the game in: game/ + screenshots/ + dossier.json
node serve.ts                             # local play-test server on http://localhost:8080
```
